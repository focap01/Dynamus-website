About Dynamus

Discovering unrealized potential, one workflow at a time.

Dynamus Training & Development Consulting, LLC was founded in 2024 by Amanda Pellum to give small and mid-sized businesses the kind of structured, custom training support usually reserved for large corporate L&D departments — without the overhead.

We specialize in instructional design, standard operating procedures, workflow development, and change management, built from a real training needs assessment rather than a generic template.

Why Dynamus is different

Most consultants hand you a plan. Dynamus builds the tools that plan calls for — custom e-learning modules, interactive job aids, and training assets designed and developed in-house using our own web and app design skill set.

  • 01

    Assess

    A comprehensive training needs assessment and gap analysis, so recommendations are based on where your team actually stands — not assumptions.

  • 02

    Design

    Custom training programs, SOPs, and job aids designed around your existing processes, in the format your team will actually open and use.

  • 03

    Build

    Working training tools — interactive e-learning modules, guides, and job aids — developed and delivered, not just outlined.

  • 04

    Support

    Train-the-trainer sessions, rollout support, and ongoing LMS or content maintenance so the program keeps working after launch.
